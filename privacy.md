# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 7, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to Chronoflux for Windows and related online services (collectively, the "Game"), operated by RegnantLilith ("Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our").

## Information We Collect

When you use the Game, we may collect and process the following information.

### Account Information

We collect your account name and identifiers associated with your Chronoflux account.

We may also maintain records of changes made to your account and account information over time. This information is used to maintain account integrity, provide Game functionality, investigate problems, and prevent abuse.

### Network and Device Information

When you connect to the Game or its online services, we may collect:

- your Internet Protocol (IP) address;
- your operating system version and other basic technical information necessary to operate and diagnose the Game; and
- approximate geographic information, such as your country or region, inferred from your IP address.

The Game does not collect precise GPS location information.

### Gameplay and Interaction Data

We collect information about your interactions with the Game. This may include gameplay events, matches, match results, hero and weapon usage, and other gameplay statistics.

We use this information to operate the Game, maintain player statistics and account information, diagnose problems, understand how the Game is being played, and improve and balance the Game.

### Game Chat

Messages sent using the Game's text chat features may be logged and retained.

Chat logs may be used for moderation, investigating player reports, preventing abuse, enforcing the Game's rules, and maintaining the safety and integrity of the Game and its community.

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information through in-game chat.

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information where necessary to:

- provide and operate the Game and its online multiplayer functionality;
- authenticate and maintain player accounts;
- provide matchmaking and multiplayer game servers;
- maintain gameplay statistics and account information;
- operate and moderate in-game communication features;
- detect, investigate, and prevent cheating, fraud, abuse, and violations of the Game's rules;
- diagnose networking problems, bugs, crashes, and other technical issues;
- analyze gameplay and improve or balance the Game;
- maintain the security and integrity of the Game and its services; and
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

We use third-party infrastructure and services where necessary to distribute and operate the Game.

### Tier.net

The Game's persistent backend infrastructure, including its account and multiplayer backend, is operated by the Service Provider on self-managed dedicated server infrastructure hosted by Tier.net in the United States.

The Service Provider administers the software and data stored on this infrastructure.

### Gameye

Gameye provides infrastructure used to host multiplayer game servers. When you participate in an online match, information necessary to establish and operate that connection, such as your IP address and gameplay/network information, may be processed through Gameye's infrastructure.

### Microsoft

Microsoft services may be used to distribute the Game through the Microsoft Store and to provide Microsoft or Xbox platform functionality.

Microsoft may process information associated with your Microsoft account, device, Store activity, or use of Microsoft platform services according to Microsoft's own privacy practices.

### Epic Online Services and Easy Anti-Cheat

If Easy Anti-Cheat or related Epic Online Services functionality is enabled in the version of the Game you use, Epic Games may process identifiers, device, technical, security, and other information necessary to provide those services, detect cheating, and protect the integrity of online gameplay.

Epic Games processes information collected through its services according to its own privacy practices.

The Game's persistent backend infrastructure is hosted on servers located in the United States.

As a result, personal information collected through the Game may be transmitted to, stored in, or processed in the United States regardless of the country from which you access the Game.

Data protection laws in these countries may differ from those in your country of residence. Where applicable law requires specific safeguards for international transfers of personal information, appropriate legally recognized safeguards will be used as required.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Information may be retained for longer where reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
